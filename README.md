## Hi, I'm Guido 👋

I build Android apps for a living, and I've been doing it for **9+ years**: from software for connected medical devices to consumer apps used at scale, like **Sisal**'s sports betting app and **Angi.com**.

- 📱 **Android first**: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Coroutines and Flow, plus a lot of legacy modernization (XML → Compose, RxJava → Coroutines)
- 🧭 **Former Mobile Team Lead** of 4 people: one-to-ones, hiring, planning and performance reviews
- 🦜 **Co-founder of [Spooky Parrots](https://spookyparrots.itch.io/)**, an indie game micro-studio: two free Unity games out, a Flutter project in the works
- 🎯 **Looking for** a senior or lead mobile role, native Android or Flutter: fully remote, or hybrid in the Marche region (Italy)
